Virfie

Enhancing remote togetherness through user-created scenarios for virtual group selfies.

Role Interaction Design · UX Research · Prototyping
Context KAIST CIxD Lab
Year 2022–2024

Overview

Virfie explores how virtual group selfies can support creative expression, social connection, and a sense of togetherness in remote settings.

Participatory Design

We explored how people wanted to create and share virtual scenarios together, using participatory design to understand opportunities for richer remote interaction.

Designing the Experience

Insights from the design process informed the interaction model and experience of creating virtual group selfies.

Building the Prototype

Virfie was developed as a working web prototype that combines real-time video communication with virtual scene composition.

Publication

Published in the Proceedings of IntelliSys 2024, with earlier work presented at CHI 2022 Extended Abstracts.
