Virfie

Enhancing remote togetherness through virtual group selfies

What happens to our sense of togetherness when being together becomes a screen?

Overview

As remote interaction becomes part of everyday life, physical presence is increasingly translated into fragments — gestures, gaze, movement, and attention mediated through a screen.

Virfie explores what is lost in this translation, and what new forms of presence might emerge from it.

Participatory Design

We conducted a participatory design process to understand how people wanted digital systems to reconstruct bodily cues, shared narratives, and micro-interactions that support social bonding.

Designing Virfie

Through iterative analysis, we identified three recurring patterns that directly informed the interaction design of Virfie.

Building the Prototype

Virfie is a WebRTC-based web application that connects users through peer-to-peer video streaming. The prototype uses real-time body segmentation to compose participants into shared virtual scenes.
